## What are these illustrations?

A personal library of AI-generated illustrations in a consistent 3D clay render style, using the Catppuccin Mocha color palette. They were originally made for use in a personal note-taking system.

## How were the illustrations made?

Each illustration was generated with [DALL-E 3 ↗](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DALL-E) using a carefully written prompt to keep the style consistent across the whole collection.

## Can I download the illustrations?

Yes. Click any illustration to open the detail view, then press **Download**.

To grab several at once, save them as favorites and use **Download favorites** in the sidebar. You'll get them as a single ZIP, up to 30 at a time.

## Can I link to an image directly?

It's better not to. Image URLs on this site are not stable and can change, disappear, or be replaced at any time. If you hotlink an image, it may break without warning. Download the illustration and host it yourself if you need a reliable copy.

## Are the illustrations free to use?

You're welcome to use them for personal projects like note-taking apps, dashboards, presentations, app icons, and similar use cases.

## How many illustrations are there?

The current count is shown at the top of the gallery. The library is still growing, so check back if you're looking for something that isn't there yet.

## Can I request an illustration?

Feel free to send your idea via the [Contribute page](/contribute). No promises, but I'd love to hear what's missing.

## What color palette do the illustrations use?

All illustrations are built on the [Catppuccin Mocha](https://catppuccin.com) palette, a dark warm-toned theme with named accent colors like Mauve, Blue, Peach, and Green. Each illustration has a dominant accent color you can filter by from the sidebar. The site uses the same palette, so the illustrations match the interface around them.

## Can I share my favorites with someone?

Yes. Save the illustrations you want to share as favorites, then use **Share favorites** in the sidebar. It generates a link that shows exactly those illustrations to anyone who opens it. The link is a snapshot of your selection at that moment. If you change your favorites later, the link will not update.

## Can I create multiple collections?

Not directly. The site supports one set of favorites at a time. As a workaround, use **Share favorites** to generate a link for each collection you want to keep, then save those links as browser bookmarks. Opening a bookmark restores that exact selection, so bookmarks work as a lightweight way to save and switch between collections. Each collection is limited to 30 illustrations.

## Why isn't my favorite illustration available in other colors?

Each illustration was generated with one or a few accent colors chosen at the time. Producing every illustration in every color combination isn't a goal. The choices were made case by case.

## Why do some illustrations look so similar?

The consistency is intentional: it's what lets them sit together in a layout without clashing. Differences in shape, color, and subject still make each one distinct.

## What if I can't find what I'm looking for?

Try the search bar. It searches by name, keyword, and tags. You can also filter by tag or accent color using the sidebar. If nothing fits, try **Random** under Display Options for some inspiration.

## Can I save illustrations for later?

Yes. Click the heart icon on any card or in the detail panel to favorite it. Use **Show favorites only** in the sidebar to see your saved illustrations.

Favorites are stored in your browser's local storage. They persist between visits on the same browser, but are not synced across devices. If you clear your browser data, your favorites will be lost.

## Is my data private?

Yes. Your favorites and display preferences are stored locally in your browser using localStorage, so nothing is sent to a server and your data never leaves your device. The site is hosted on Vercel, which logs basic request data (IP address, browser type). It also uses Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. No cookies are set, no personal data is collected, and nothing is shared with third parties.
