## Search

Type anything to filter the gallery by name, tag, or keyword. Typos are fine. Click the × inside the search field or press **Escape** to clear it.

## Favorites

Click the heart on any illustration to save it. Use **Show favorites only** to see just your saved ones. Use **Remove favorites** to clear them.

Use **Download favorites** to save all your favorites as a single ZIP file. Download is limited to 30 favorites at a time.

## Tags & accent colors

Pick one or more tags or accent colors to filter the gallery. When two or more are selected, a toggle appears to switch between matching any or all of them. The reset button next to each section clears that filter.

## Sorting

Under **Display Options** in the sidebar, switch between **Alphabetical** (default) and **Random**. Pressing **Random** again re-shuffles and opens the first result.

## Card size & layout

Use **Small / Medium / Large** to change card size. **Grid Width** switches between Narrow, Wide, and Full. Both are under Display Options in the sidebar.

## Detail view

Click any illustration to open the detail panel on the right. It shows the full image, tags, metadata, and download options. Click any tag or accent color in the panel to add it as a filter. Use **Copy link** to share the current view. Use **Previous** and **Next** at the bottom to browse without closing.

Click outside the panel or press **Escape** to close it.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| `/` or `S` | Focus the search bar |
| `Escape` | Clear search (in search bar) or close the detail panel |
| `F` | Toggle favorite on active or focused illustration |
| `V` | Toggle favorites-only view |
| `D` | Download active or focused illustration |
| `R` | Open a random illustration |
| `←` / `→` | Previous / Next illustration (panel open) |
| `↑` / `↓` | Jump up / down a row (panel open) |
| Arrow keys | Move focus in the grid |
| `Enter` / `Space` | Open focused card |

## Download

Open an illustration and click **Download** (or press `D`) to save it to your device. To grab several at once, save them as favorites and use **Download favorites** (see above).
